**Primary Drivers of Decoupling:**

1. **Data Security and Privacy**: Centralized systems are vulnerable to data breaches, surveillance, and unauthorized access. The decoupling of compute models allows for the creation of localized, air-gapped networks that are less susceptible to these threats.

2. **Regulatory Compliance**: The increasing need for data sovereignty and compliance with local regulatory frameworks has driven the adoption of localized compute models. These models allow organizations to manage data within their own jurisdictions, reducing dependency on cloud providers.

3. **Autonomy and Independence**: Localized compute models provide organizations with greater autonomy and independence in their data management and processing decisions.
