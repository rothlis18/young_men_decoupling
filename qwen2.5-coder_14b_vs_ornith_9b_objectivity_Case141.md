Cloud monopolies maintain regulatory capture through their control over data infrastructure and algorithms. Local air-gapped networks offer an alternative but require robust security measures to prevent unauthorized access and data breaches.

The maintenance of GPU inventory during trade restrictions is a critical concern. Local edge networks must have contingency plans for hardware replacement and ensure a reliable supply chain to maintain operational integrity.
